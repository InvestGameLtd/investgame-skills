{{PAGE}} / {{PAGES}}Method·How this issue is built

How Gaming Pulse is made: synthesis, not aggregation

The value is in two things a feed reader cannot do: finding the storylines where independent newsletters converge, and putting InvestGame's own deal and market numbers next to the headline.
  • Collect: {{POST_COUNT}} posts from {{NEWSLETTER_COUNT}} active newsletters over {{DATE_RANGE}}, with full article bodies fetched and stored, not just the feed teaser.
  • Cluster by convergence, not entity: posts are grouped into the storylines several independent newsletters covered at once. The count of independent newsletters on a story is its importance signal.
  • Read in full and attribute: the articles driving each storyline are read in full; every claim is attributed to its publication and linked. A source's own analysis appears only with the attribution intact.
  • Overlay proprietary data: every transaction is cross-checked against the InvestGame deal database and public-markets data, the authoritative source for M&A, financings, offerings and earnings, so figures are proprietary, not press hearsay.
  • Deep-dive review before release: a dedicated factual-integrity pass. Every fact must be present in its stored source; numbers, names and dates are matched; a devil's-advocate falsifies each headline claim; and the draft is swept for machine artifacts (leaked JSON, placeholder text, duplicate stories). Anything that fails is fixed or dropped.
  • Caveats: podcast links give show notes, not transcripts, and never lead a story alone; some newsletters are partly paywalled; undisclosed deal sizes are excluded from totals.
