# InvestGame - research sources (priority order)

When a deep report or research task needs outside context and narrative, gather in this order. Always start
with InvestGame's own content, then lean on the curated gaming sources before generic web results.

## 1 · InvestGame first - investgame.net
Search investgame.net first. Beyond the deal / financing / M&A data, InvestGame publishes business and
strategy coverage - private-equity and investment-activity reports, quarterly reviews, and corporate news.
Most topics already have useful InvestGame context - grab it before going outside.

## 2 · Curated gaming-industry sources (prefer these over generic search)
Trusted analysts, newsletters and data houses. Pick by topic - the focus note tells you who to reach for:
- investgame.net - InvestGame: gaming deals, financings and M&A, plus business & strategy reports,
  private-equity / investment-activity coverage and quarterly reviews (the home dataset; corporate- and
  business-news oriented).
- newzoo.com - Newzoo: market sizing, player numbers & preferences, audience and revenue forecasts -  strong on the PC/console side.
- substack.nikopartners.com - Niko Partners: Asia, MENA and emerging-market games.
- mobilegamerbiz.substack.com - Mobilegamer.biz: mobile games business.
- deconstructoroffun.com - Mishka Katkoff: free-to-play / mobile design, monetization, and deconstructions
  of top-performing games.
- naavik.co - Naavik: games-industry research, consulting and strategy - business, strategy and
  strategic-advisory coverage.
- konvoy.vc - Konvoy Ventures: gaming-VC data, theses and weekly newsletter.
- superjoost.substack.com - Joost van Dreunen: general games-industry news & analysis, broad and
  cross-platform.
- thegamebusiness.com - Christopher Dring: games-business news.
- gamefile.news - Stephen Totilo: games-industry news & reporting.
- newsletter.gamediscover.co - Simon Carless: PC/console games (indie, AA, AAA) - discoverability &
  platform data.
- alineaanalytics.substack.com - Rhys Elliott: PC/console games & market analysis.
- gamedevreports.substack.com - Dmitriy Byshonkov: industry reports & data analytics - the strongest report
  aggregator after InvestGame.
- newsletter.hushcrasher.com - Hush Crasher: industry commentary.
- blog.davidkaye.co - David Kaye (F4 Fund): early-stage gaming-VC perspective.
- matthewball.co - Matthew Ball: strategy & long-form industry essays.

## 3 · General web
For broader narrative and context - company sites, reputable press, primary announcements. Cite everything;
when sources disagree on a number, show the range rather than silently picking one.

## 4 · Source traps (avoid the common error class)
- **Crunchbase investor attribution.** Crunchbase lists ALL investors a company has ever had, not just the
  participants in the round you are looking at. Attribute an investor to a round only when a source confirms
  they joined *that* round - never assume the full investor list backed the latest raise.
- **Round-level transitions.** A later round (Seed → A → B → …) does not re-credit the earlier-round leads.
  Read each round's participants separately; don't carry the Series A lead into the Series B count.
