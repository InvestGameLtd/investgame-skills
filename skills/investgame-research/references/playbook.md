# InvestGame - research & report playbook

The method behind the hub: the standard data cuts, the report-type recipes, and how to size and structure a
deliverable. Pull this when you're planning or building a deep report.

## Standard data cuts (the comprehensive picture)
Unless the user narrows the ask, a deep report assembles several cuts so the story is rounded - all via
the investgame-gaming-data hub:
- Top deals - the ~15 largest relevant deals (M&A and/or financings), with the key terms.
- Top players - the ~15 most active acquirers / investors, or the most-funded companies.
- Historical dynamics - time series across dimensions:
  - Value - deal value over time.
  - Count - number of deals / events over time.
  - Valuation - multiples over time (where disclosed).
- Breakdowns - by segment, platform, geography or deal type, as the topic needs.
Each cut earns its place; don't make two cuts say the same thing. (These are aggregate/time-series pulls,
which the InvestGame chat answers directly.)

## Report-type recipes (sections to pick from)
- Market / regional / segment - market at a glance (KPIs) · deal activity & trend · segment/genre breakdown
  · notable companies · benchmark vs peers / global · grounded outlook.
- Deep dive / trend - the question · the trend over time (value / count / valuation) · what's driving it ·
  who's involved · what it means.
- Company / deal - profile · funding & deal history · valuation context & comparable deals · positioning ·
  outlook.
- Investor / fund - activity & cadence · portfolio profile (stage, geography, segment) · funds & AUM ·
  notable deals.
- Meeting / call prep - who they are · recent deals & funding · how they compare to peers · likely topics
  and the open questions to walk in with. (Lighter than a full report - Format may deliver this as a brief,
  not a deck.)
- Meeting / call notes (from a transcript) - participants · summary (decisions, not topics) · discussion
  by topic · InvestGame context on the companies/deals mentioned · action items (single owner + deadline) ·
  open questions. Processed from the transcript, not plan-first (see the notes mode in the skill).

## Sizing & structure
- Plan first: a short TOC - ~5 sections for a focused ask, up to ~15 for a broad one. Confirm before
  building, and ask which theme (Warm White or Dark Navy).
- Benchmark, don't describe - every section adds context, not just a list. A market or company shown alone
  is a list; in context it's an insight.
- Market sizing (InvestGame doesn't hold it): third-party sources, cited separately, averaged with the range
  shown - never blended into InvestGame deal numbers.
- Honest gaps: "insufficient public data" beats an invented number. Keep the outlook grounded - no hype.
