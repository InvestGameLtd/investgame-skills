# Research playbook - the data-vs-web division of labour

Shared by the `investgame-research` spoke (deep reports, company/investor profiles, meeting/call prep and
transcript notes). It states **who researches what** and the **source catalogue** to pull from. Load it
whenever a task needs research beyond InvestGame's own deal data.

## Division of labour (the defining principle)

InvestGame supplies **two things**: the gaming **deal/market data** (via the `InvestGame_query` MCP) and
the **playbooks** (these skills). **InvestGame does not browse the web.** When a task needs supplementary
research - a founder's background, a company's app-store traction, the latest news, third-party
market-size figures - **the client's own agent runs that research with its own tools.** The skill's job
is to say *exactly what to find, where to look, and how to present it.* If the agent has no web
capability, say so plainly and deliver only the InvestGame-data layers rather than leaving blanks.

## Source catalogue (pull the rows the task needs)

| Layer | Who supplies | Where to look |
|-------|--------------|---------------|
| **Deal / funding / M&A / valuation / comps** | **InvestGame** (the hub) | `InvestGame_query` - a generic web search can't give this |
| **Company** | you (web) | official site / about; **App Store + Google Play + Steam** (products, ratings, chart rank as traction proxies); LinkedIn company page (HQ, headcount, founded); Crunchbase/PitchBook (funding cross-check); public filings if listed |
| **People** (per named attendee) | you (web) | LinkedIn profile (role, tenure, prior roles, education) + recent public activity (~12 months: posts/reposts, interviews, podcasts, talks) - present **neutrally**, never as angles to use on them |
| **News** | you (web) | reputable gaming/business outlets, last 6–12 months: launches, raises, hires, awards, strategic moves |
| **Market size** (revenue, players) | you (web) | third-party sources (e.g. Newzoo, Statista, local-market reports) - **cite each separately, average across sources and show the range** when they disagree; never blend into InvestGame deal numbers |

## Sourcing honesty

Flag anything you can't confirm as "could not verify"; never invent a figure or a fact. Keep the
InvestGame deal layer and your web layer clearly labelled so the reader always knows which is which.
