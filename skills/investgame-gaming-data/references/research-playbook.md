# Research playbook - the data-vs-web division of labour

Used by the hub for any task that needs research beyond InvestGame's own data (deep reports,
company and investor profiles, meeting and call prep, transcript notes). It states **who researches
what** and **where to look**. The priority list of outside sources is
`references/research-sources.md` in `investgame-research`.

## Division of labour (the defining principle)

InvestGame supplies **two things**: the gaming **deal, company and market data** (through the
InvestGame tools the hub routes to) and the **playbooks** (these skills). **InvestGame does not
browse the web.** When a task needs supplementary research - a founder's background, a company's
app-store traction, the latest news, third-party market-size figures - **the client's own agent runs
that research with its own tools.** The skill's job is to say *exactly what to find, where to look,
and how to present it.* If the agent has no web capability, say so plainly and deliver only the
InvestGame-data layers rather than leaving blanks.

## Source catalogue (pull the rows the task needs)

| Layer | Who supplies | Where to look |
|-------|--------------|---------------|
| **Deal / funding / M&A / valuation / comps** | **InvestGame** | `InvestGame_query` - a generic web search can't give this |
| **Company** | **InvestGame** first, then you (web) | `InvestGame_company_query` for headcount and its history, key people and ownership; then the web for what the card does not hold: official site, **App Store + Google Play + Steam** (products, ratings, chart rank as traction proxies), public filings if listed |
| **People** (per named attendee) | you (web) | key people come from the company card; the web adds professional background (role, tenure, prior roles, education) and recent public activity (~12 months: posts, interviews, podcasts, talks) - present **neutrally**, never as angles to use on them |
| **News** | you (web) | reputable gaming and business outlets, last 6-12 months: launches, raises, hires, awards, strategic moves; `investgame-press` for what the gaming newsletters wrote |
| **Market size** (revenue, players) | you (web) | third-party market research - **cite each source separately, average across sources and show the range** when they disagree; never blend into InvestGame deal numbers |

## Sourcing honesty

Flag anything you can't confirm as "could not verify"; never invent a figure or a fact. Keep the
InvestGame layer and your web layer clearly labelled so the reader always knows which is which.
